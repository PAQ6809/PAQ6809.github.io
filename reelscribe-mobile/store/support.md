# ReelScribe Support

## Getting started

1. Paste a public YouTube or Instagram link, or select a local video/audio file.
2. Choose the spoken language when known.
3. Use Tiny for speed and long videos; use Base for balanced quality.
4. Start transcription and keep the app open for the first release unless the screen says the job may continue in the background.
5. Review the transcript before exporting TXT, SRT or VTT.

## Common issues

### A public link does not work

The platform may not expose captions or anonymous public media. Private, login-only, paid, removed, regional, age-restricted or DRM content is unsupported. Use a local file you are authorized to process.

### The transcript repeats words or symbols

Confirm the language, enable speech enhancement for spoken videos and try a larger approved model. Music-only or unclear audio may be rejected instead of producing guessed text.

### OCR returns no text

OCR reads visible burned-in captions only. Increase the reading area, choose the correct language and use a frame where the subtitles are large and clear.

### Model download stops

Check free space, network stability, Low Power Mode and thermal state. The app keeps partial files separate and must verify the completed model before use.

### The app closes during a long video

Use Tiny or Base, close other memory-heavy apps and retry from the saved checkpoint after the native resume flow is enabled in the release build.

## Privacy

Local mode keeps selected media, decoded audio, OCR frames and transcripts on the device. Public-link requests send the public URL to the resolver but do not include social passwords, cookies or private tokens.
