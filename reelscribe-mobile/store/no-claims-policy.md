# Marketing Claim Guardrails

Approved language:

- `公開字幕優先`
- `本機 AI 字幕`
- `最佳努力解析公開連結`
- `支援 TXT、SRT、VTT`
- `本機模式不主動上傳影片`
- `依裝置能力選擇模型`

Prohibited without reproducible evidence:

- `100% 準確`
- `所有 Instagram／YouTube 連結都能用`
- `完全去除背景音樂`
- `即時處理所有長影片`
- `永久保證免費`
- `不會當機`
- `最先進／世界第一` without a dated, scoped benchmark
- Claims of partnership or endorsement by Instagram, YouTube, Apple, Google, OpenAI, Hugging Face or model authors

Every store, website and social post must identify private, restricted, paywalled, regional and DRM limitations where relevant.
