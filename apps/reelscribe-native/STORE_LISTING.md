**Review notes draft:**

- The app does not require an account.
- Local files are transcribed on the device after the user explicitly chooses a model.
- OCR reads only frames from the user-selected local video and does not upload screenshots.
- Public-link lookup uses public URLs only and does not accept social account cookies or credentials.
- Private, login-only, DRM-protected, age-restricted, and region-restricted media are unsupported.
- A sample local video and a public-caption test URL must be supplied during review.

## Google Play

**Short description:** Create editable subtitles from public captions or local videos with on-device AI.

**Full description:**

ReelScribe helps you create editable subtitles from authorized public video links and videos stored on your device. It first checks for public caption tracks. When no public captions are available, supported devices can use local speech recognition or local screen OCR.

Key features:
- Local speech-to-text after an optional model download
- Optional OCR for text visibly burned into video frames
- Public caption lookup without social account passwords or cookies
- Editable transcript and timestamp timeline
- Export TXT, SRT, and VTT
- No account required

Important limitations:
- Results depend on audio quality, language, device capability, and whether a platform exposes public captions or public media.
- Private, login-only, DRM-protected, removed, age-restricted, and region-restricted content is not bypassed.
- Users are responsible for having permission to process the content.
